Hypotheses to Test

  • 1
    Higher effort-inequality → more normative conflicts (H1)
  • 2
    Punishment only effective when norms are homogeneous (H2)
  • 3
    With norm learning, one norm eventually dominates (H3)
  • 4
    More content conflicts than commitment conflicts in mixed populations
